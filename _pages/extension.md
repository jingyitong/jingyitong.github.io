### Women Landowners' Voices in Iowa

Women own nearly half of all farmland acres in Iowa and are an essential group in agriculture and natural resources. This project develops tailored educational materials to improve the knowledge and confidence of women landowners on farmland leasing, soil and water conservation practices, and farmland transition planning.

- **Award:** 2023 AAEA Graduate Student Extension Competition, Second Place
- **Publications:** Tong et al. (2024), _Renewable Agriculture and Food Systems_; Schultz et al. (2022), _Journal of the NACAA_; Tong and Zhang (2022), _CARD Policy Brief_
- **Presentations:** Extension Risk Management National Conference (April 2024); Women in Ag Leadership Conference (November 2022)

### Iowa Farmland Ownership and Tenure Survey

Using a statistically representative sample of Iowa landowners in 2022, this project provides a 40-year perspective (1982 to 2022) on land ownership, tenure, acquisition, succession, and farmland characteristics. The 2022 survey also examines in-field and edge-of-field conservation practices, trust usage, and potential transfers to beginning farmers.

- **Publications:** Tong and Zhang (2023), _Iowa State University Extension and Outreach_, FM 1893; Hart, Tong, and Zhang (2023), _Agricultural Policy Review_
- **Presentation:** Iowa Nutrient Research Center (October 2023)

### Mesotunnel Row Covers for Organic Cucurbit Growers

This study assesses the adoption potential of mesotunnels, medium-sized breathable nylon-mesh structures, among organic cucurbit growers. Applying the Technology Acceptance Model 3 (TAM3) and ordered logit models to 337 survey responses, we find that a marginal increase in perceived efficacy or ease of use raises the likelihood of adoption by 1.55 and 2.34 times, respectively.

- **Publications:** [Wehner, J., Tong, J., Zhang, W., Li, T., Pulliam, K., Gonthier, D., Sciligo, A., & Miller, E. (2025)](https://dx.doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5767702)<br>, _Cornell SC Johnson College of Business Research Paper_
- **Presentation:** AFHVS/ASFS Conference (June 2024)
